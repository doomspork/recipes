directions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 325°F. Finely process the onion and garlic in a food processor. Combine with the ground lamb, ground beef, breadcrumbs, oregano, cumin, salt, Aleppo pepper, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly until the mixture is dense and well combined."
  - "Press the meat mixture firmly into a 9x5 inch loaf pan, removing any air pockets. Bake for 50-60 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F."
  - "Remove from oven and let rest for 20 minutes. For best results, refrigerate the loaf until firm, which makes slicing easier."
  - "Remove the loaf from the pan and cut into thin 1/8-inch strips."
  - "Heat a griddle or skillet over medium-high heat with a small amount of the reserved meat drippings. Cook the sliced meat for 1-2 minutes per side until browned and slightly crispy on the edges."
  - "Serve the meat in warmed pita bread topped with tzatziki sauce, sliced tomatoes, red onion, chopped lettuce, and feta cheese."
