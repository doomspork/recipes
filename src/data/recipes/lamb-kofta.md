directions:
  - "Combine all yoghurt sauce ingredients in a bowl and refrigerate for at least 20 minutes to allow the flavours to develop. Overnight is even better."
  - "Grate the onion directly into a large bowl using a box grater. Add the remaining kofta ingredients and mix thoroughly with your hands until well combined."
  - "Divide the meat into 8 equal pieces. With damp hands, shape each piece and push onto a skewer, forming an elongated oval shape."
  - "Heat 1/2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook kofta in batches for about 5 minutes total, turning to brown evenly on all sides. Alternatively, grill on a BBQ over medium-high heat for 6 minutes, or broil 10 inches from the heat source for 12 minutes, rotating as needed."
  - "Serve by smearing warmed flatbread with yoghurt sauce, topping with shredded salad and a kofta skewer, then drizzling with extra sauce."
