title: "Mexican Shredded Chicken"
servings: "6 Servings"
prep_time: "10 minutes"
cook_time: "4 hours"
total_time: "4h 10m"
tags:
  - "Slow Cooker"
  - "Chicken"
  - "Dinner"
  - "Mexican"
ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Chicken & Sauce"
    items:
      - amount: "1 1/2 lbs"
        name: "boneless skinless chicken breast (about 3 halves)"
      - amount: "1/2 cup"
        name: "chipotles in adobo sauce (about 4 chiles plus sauce)"
      - amount: "14 oz"
        name: "can crushed tomatoes"
      - amount: "2"
        name: "garlic cloves, crushed"
      - amount: "1 tsp"
        name: "dried oregano"
      - amount: "1 tsp"
        name: "ground cumin"
      - amount: "1 tsp"
        name: "onion powder (or garlic powder)"
      - amount: "2 tsp"
        name: "sugar (any type)"
      - amount: "3/4 tsp"
        name: "salt"
      - name: "black pepper, to taste"
  - name: "To Finish"
    items:
      - amount: "3 tbsp"
        name: "olive oil"
      - amount: "2 tbsp"
        name: "lime juice"
directions:
  - "Place the chicken, chipotles in adobo, crushed tomatoes, garlic, oregano, cumin, onion powder, sugar, salt, and a few grinds of pepper in the slow cooker. Stir to combine and coat the chicken."
  - "Cover and cook on low for 4 hours. The chicken is ready when it shreds easily with two forks but is not mushy — overcooked chicken turns dry and stringy, so check it toward the end."
  - "Transfer the chicken to a plate and shred with two forks. Set aside."
  - "Add the lime juice and 1 tbsp of the olive oil to the sauce left in the slow cooker. Blend with a stick blender (or transfer to a regular blender) until smooth — you should have about 2 cups of sauce. Taste and adjust salt and pepper."
  - "Optional, for extra flavor: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add 1/3 to 1/2 of the shredded chicken and fry until deep golden brown on one side, then flip and cook the other side very briefly (don't brown it). Remove and repeat with the remaining chicken, using 1 tbsp oil per batch."
  - "Toss the chicken through the sauce, or serve the sauce on the side. Use it for tacos, enchiladas, burritos, quesadillas, sliders, or piled over rice."
