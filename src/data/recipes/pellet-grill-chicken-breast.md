---
title: "Pellet Grill Chicken Breast"
servings: "6 Servings"
prep_time: "15 minutes"
cook_time: "35 minutes"
total_time: "50m"
tags:
  - "Pellet Grill"
  - "Chicken"
  - "Dinner"
  - "American"
ingredients:
  - amount: "6"
    name: "boneless, skinless chicken breasts"
  - amount: "2 tbsp"
    name: '<a href="../pork-and-chicken-rub/">Pork and Chicken Rub</a>'
  - amount: "1 tbsp"
    name: "olive oil (or avocado oil)"
  - amount: "1 cup"
    name: "BBQ sauce (optional)"
directions:
  - "Preheat pellet grill to 325°F."
  - "Coat chicken breasts with olive oil, then apply the rub generously to all sides."
  - "Place seasoned chicken directly on the grill grates, close the lid, and cook for 15 minutes."
  - "Flip the chicken and cook for an additional 10 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 150°F."
  - "Baste each breast with BBQ sauce and cook for another 5-10 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 160°F."
  - "Remove the chicken to a plate and tent with foil. Allow to rest until the internal temperature reaches 165°F."
  - "Serve with extra BBQ sauce on the side if desired."
