---
title: "Smoked Pulled Pork"
servings: "16 Servings"
prep_time: "15 minutes"
cook_time: "18 hours"
rest_time: "1 hour"
total_time: "19h 15m"
tags:
  - "Smoker"
  - "Pork"
  - "Dinner"
  - "American"
ingredients:
  - amount: "8-10 lb"
    name: "bone-in pork shoulder (Boston butt)"
  - amount: "2-3 tbsp"
    name: "yellow mustard"
  - amount: "1/4 cup"
    name: "sweet BBQ rub, plus extra for serving"
directions:
  - "Preheat smoker to 225°F using hickory or apple wood."
  - "Wipe down the pork shoulder with paper towels to remove any bone fragments and excess moisture."
  - "Coat the entire exterior of the pork shoulder liberally with yellow mustard as a binder."
  - "Season generously on all sides with sweet BBQ rub until the shoulder is fully coated."
  - "Place the pork shoulder fat-side up in the center of the grate, away from any hot spots. Close the lid and smoke for 15-20 hours until the internal temperature reaches 195-201°F. Plan for approximately 2 hours per pound. The temperature will stall around 145-165°F — this is normal. Do not wrap; this builds the caramelized bark."
  - "Remove from smoker and wrap tightly in aluminum foil. Rest for at least 1 hour."
  - "Pull the pork apart using forks or meat shredder claws, discarding any fat and gristle. Sprinkle extra rub over the finished pork before serving."
