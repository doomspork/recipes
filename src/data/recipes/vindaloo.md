directions:
  - "Toss the beef cubes with salt in a large bowl and set aside."
  - "Make the curry paste by blending the Kashmiri chilli powder, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, cloves, cardamom pods, cinnamon, peppercorns, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, fenugreek seeds, turmeric, and white vinegar with 1 cup of water until smooth."
  - "Pour the curry paste over the beef, add the remaining 1 cup of water, and stir to combine. Cover and marinate for 2 hours in the fridge."
  - "Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C conventional, 340°F/170°C fan-forced)."
  - "Melt the ghee in a large oven-safe pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion, ginger, and garlic and cook for about 3 minutes until translucent."
  - "Add the mustard seeds and cook until the onion turns golden. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the beef stock, then blend the sauce smooth using a stick blender."
  - "Add the marinated beef along with all the marinade and the curry leaves. Stir and bring to a boil."
  - "Cover with a lid and transfer to the oven. Bake for 2 hours until the beef is fall-apart tender and the sauce has darkened in color."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve over basmati rice with naan."
